OUR APPROACH / 03

Numbers are useful.
People are the point.

Clarity over complexity

A few genuinely useful insights beat dozens of disconnected charts.

Care over chasing metrics

Recorded outreach sources and visitor returns support decisions. They don't prove what caused someone's journey.

Privacy from the beginning

The current prototype uses fictional IDs and local browser storage. Secure production accounts and real member data handling are not yet implemented.
