## A. Website privacy notice — draft for fictional prototype

**Who we are.** This website presents a developing church outreach and growth analytics product. The legal entity operating the service and privacy contact will be identified in the approved publication version.

**What is currently processed.** The public demonstration uses fictional campaigns and pseudonymous sample visitor identifiers held in browser-local storage. We do not currently invite or permit users to enter identifiable church member information. When visitors access the site, hosting and third-party providers may receive technical network information such as IP address, browser details and request metadata. The site may fetch fonts or illustrative photographs from external providers; these connections need verification in a browser audit.

**Why.** Displaying the website, operating the fictional interactive demonstration, responding to lawful enquiries if a channel is launched, maintaining service availability and preventing misuse. Do not assert a final legal basis until target-country review.

**Who receives information.** The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. At the time of drafting, the website source includes remote Google Fonts and Unsplash image links; identify all final recipients, their locations and contracts before publication. The Neon staging database is not connected to the public fictional demonstration.

**Storage and retention.** Fictional demo changes persist in local browser storage until the visitor clears site data or chooses Reset Demo. The host and third-party providers can keep technical logs according to their own arrangements; request or verify retention schedules before publishing a definitive notice.

**Your rights.** The final notice must explain applicable rights, how to exercise them, response times, regulator contact and cross-border processing information for each launch location.

**Safety.** Do not input names, telephone numbers, email addresses, religious beliefs or identifiable member details into the public demo. Production privacy and security controls are still under development.

## B. Cookies and similar storage notice — draft

We need to distinguish *essential local storage* from optional analytics/marketing storage, and distinguish HTTP cookies from other browser storage.

**Current confirmed application-level storage:** `localStorage` key `church-growth-focused-mvp-v1` stores fictional demo modifications and enables them to remain when the page reloads. Visitors can remove this through the app's Reset Demo control or their browser's website-data controls.

**Further verification required:** hosting-layer cookies, remote font and image requests, authentication session cookies when sign-in is added, any analytics added in future, storage purpose, lifetime and third-party access. Inspect through browser developer tools and network logs before any claim of "no cookies" or "only essential cookies."

**Consent / settings principle:** if non-essential cookies or comparable tracking are introduced, obtain any consent required by applicable law before loading them, with an equally usable decline path and a way to revisit settings. Do not show misleading buttons or claim consent is given by continuing to browse.

**Draft cookie settings UI:** When no optional cookies are deployed, show an accessible short explanation and a "Reset demonstration data" link. Add actual category toggles *only* when functional optional technologies exist and can be blocked prior to consent. Record the cookie policy version and review changes whenever vendors/scripts change.
